How to play.

Get to the mansion flag. Roadside turntables save your place during the run.

Move
← → or A / D
Duck
↓ or S. Hold Down on the directional pad.
Jump
Space, ↑ or W. Hold to jump higher.
Speed
Hold Shift while moving.
Lift / throw
Stomp a rival, then press X nearby to lift it. Press X again to throw.
Pause
P or Escape

Touch players can slide around the four-way pad and tap an action with the other thumb. Up also jumps. Tap Speed to switch running on or off. Head-bump a gold record crate for eight seconds of Encore power.
