Returnity starter pack for Sound Arcade v0.3.1
==============================================

Nige Hood's settings and artwork, kept apart from the kit so the kit stays generic.

To use it, copy into an unzipped Sound Arcade folder:
  site.config.json        -> replaces the kit's site.config.json
  assets/uploads/*        -> into the kit's assets/uploads/
  apps/                   -> merge into the kit's apps/ (adds Highway Encore to the arcade shelf)

Then npm start, check the Home page section, add his songs to music/, and build.

What's in the settings
- Title RETURN / ITY, neutral dark theme with one gold accent (#e2ad4f). Colors are not final.
- Route D.C. -> Charlotte -> Atlanta along I-95/I-85, Raleigh as a light only, Atlanta brightest,
  return arc on, US East Coast outline.
- Stop text is all marked Draft, for Nige to replace in his own words:
    D.C.       "D.C. is where it all started."
    Charlotte  "My family stood behind me on my musical journey."
    Atlanta    "Atlanta always believed in me."
- Eras 2008 / 2014 / 2020 / 2026 (2008 in black and white, 2026 in the gold glow).
- mural.webp (hero artwork) and nige-cutout.webp (character cutout).
  The cutout is a stand-in until his final art (gold hair and aura, his swirl jacket in gold and white).
- Highway Encore (game workshop v3.1) on the arcade shelf, first in line, with its controls listed
  for the Controls panel (Select opens Music). Its pause screen has a Music button that opens the
  arcade's music player.
