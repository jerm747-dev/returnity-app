Control room

Move with the left stick or WASD. Aim with the right stick or arrow keys. Space fires. Crosshairs follow the ship by default; change this below. View and controller buttons remain available before launch.

Flight continues from the same position when you close this panel. Hold each stick in turn to test it. Physical controllers may need a button press to appear.

Input mode

Simple mode: use the large center stick or controller left stick to aim; the ship follows. Right stick is unused. Focus assist has twice the grace and can acquire weapon pickups. Fire is still manual.

Mouse mode: move over the flight field to place the reticle. The ship drifts after it, trailing slightly below. No mouse click is needed. Space still fires; moving outside the flight field stops the mouse follow.

Columns: action · mouse in right hand · mouse in left hand. Choose a shortcut to change it. These bindings only apply in mouse mode; keyboard / twin-stick mode keeps its current mapping.

Aiming behavior

0.90

Small stick movements make fine adjustments. Push farther for larger sweeps. This setting also adjusts keyboard aiming.

Release the aiming stick near a rock to acquire it. Systems briefly hold within a 10 m focus area. Move the aiming stick to take over immediately.

Carry the aim at the same distance and angle. At the field edge, the reticle swings inward without restricting the ship. That adjusted angle stays until you aim again.

0.75 s

In follow mode, the reticle drifts toward its intended position. This is the approximate time to settle after you stop. Zero restores instant following.
