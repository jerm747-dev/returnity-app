Find your rhythm.

A quiet block to explore. Take the sidewalk, hop onto the crates, or climb the awnings and balconies. The camera follows you upward.

Move
← → or A / D
Jump
Space, ↑ or W. Release early for a shorter jump.
Flip
X. Start a tucked jump from the ground, or tuck once during a regular jump.
Speed
Hold Shift, or tap Speed to switch running on/off.
Duck
↓ or S
Pause
P or Escape

Walk near the person with the speech bubble to stop for a short chat. Everyone else keeps moving. Jump past them or choose Keep walking to skip. Finish the conversation to receive one merchant token for this run.

Touch: slide around the eight-way D-pad and use Jump or Flip with your other thumb. Upper corners jump and move together.
