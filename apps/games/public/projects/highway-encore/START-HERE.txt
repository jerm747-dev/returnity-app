HIGHWAY ENCORE — STREET SESSION
Game workshop revision 5 / compatible with the v3 player-page pair

OPEN THE DEMO
Open index.html in this folder. Choose Start walking.
For a website, upload the entire highway-encore folder beside the existing
player page. No build, installation, external font, or CDN is required.
All art and code are included; the game can also run from local files.

WHAT THIS TEST DOES
Explore one calm street with four moving pedestrians and no enemies.
Walk, run, jump, duck, or make a tucked front flip. Climb floating wooden
steps, awnings, balconies, and the rooftop. The camera follows vertically.
The earlier broad-band gold-crate design is used for two reward blocks.
Bump a gold crate from below to get five notes; each rewards only once.
The end-of-block sign does not stop play: turn back and keep exploring.

ONE OPTIONAL ENCOUNTER
One of the first two pedestrians is selected at the start of each run.
A speech bubble marks that person. Approach along the ground to chat.
Nige and this person stop; all other pedestrians keep moving.
Left/right portrait cards show the latest line from each participant.
Finish the six-line open-mic conversation to receive one merchant token.
Choose Keep walking or press Escape to leave early without a reward.
Jump past the marked person or use an upper route to skip the encounter.
Once completed, left, or bypassed, it will not interrupt that run again.
The token is a demonstration counter. The merchant shop is not built yet.
Tokens, notes, and encounter progress reset with a new run or reload.

KEYBOARD
Left/Right or A/D: move
Up, W, or Space: regular jump; hold for more height
Shift: hold to run
X: tucked front-flip jump, or tuck once during a regular jump
Down or S: duck
P: pause/resume
Escape: leave the conversation, otherwise pause/resume
Enter/Space: activate the focused conversation button

TOUCH / POINTER
Slide around the circular eight-way pad to change direction.
Upper diagonals jump and move; lower diagonals duck and move.
Both matching caps and the ring segment light up for diagonals.
The center is neutral. Releasing the pad clears its inputs.
The action arc has Speed, Flip, and Jump. Speed is a tap-on/tap-off latch.
Use the pad and action buttons together with multiple fingers.
Inputs clear on pause, dialogue, loss of focus, or a new run.
Physical gamepad support is not included.

ART AND ANIMATION STATUS
The green outfit, white tee, and white sneakers are now in the game.
First 16-frame walk and 16-frame run studies are included as PNG atlases.
These are workshop animation studies, with room for gait/spacing cleanup.
The regular jump uses one pose. The flip rotates the tucked pose once;
it is not a hand-drawn 16-frame jump or flip animation yet.
Platforms are placed against the painted street artwork. Some scenery
people in that artwork are static; the four foreground walkers animate.
Sound is optional synthesized effects, not album audio.
