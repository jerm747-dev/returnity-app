How to play.

Get to the mansion flag. Roadside turntables save your place during the run.

Move
← → or A / D
Duck
↓ or S. Hold Down on the directional pad.
Jump
Space, ↑ or W. Hold to jump higher.
Speed
Hold Shift while moving.
Lift / throw
Stomp a rival, then press X nearby to lift it. Press X again to throw.
Pause
P or Escape

Slide around the circular eight-way pad. Corners combine directions: upper corners jump and move; lower corners duck and move. Both direction caps and the matching ring segment light up. The center is neutral. Up also jumps. Tap Speed to switch running on or off. Head-bump a gold record crate for eight seconds of Encore power.
